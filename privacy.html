1. 100% On-Device & Privacy-First Architecture

Saksham is dedicated to empowering individuals living with Parkinson’s disease and neuro-cognitive impairments. We believe health and cognitive adherence data is deeply personal. Therefore, Saksham operates on a client-first, local-storage architecture. All your completed routines, daily calendar timestamps, Level XP progress, loved ones' contact entries, and doctor telemetry directives are stored directly inside your device's secure browser storage (HTML5 LocalStorage).

🛡️ Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your cognitive assessments, routine logs, or personal identity to any advertising network, third-party broker, or marketing entity.

2. Device Permissions & Why They Are Requested

🎤 Microphone (Audio Input): Used solely for the optional LSVT LOUD Speech Volume Drill. Audio decibel levels are processed transiently in real time via the Web Audio API to estimate vocal projection loudness. No voice audio is ever recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.
📷 Camera (Visual Input): Used optionally in the Loved Ones & Face Recall Hub to practice recognizing familiar family members and caregivers. Image frames are processed locally in your browser's canvas memory. No images or facial scans leave your personal device.
🔔 Audio Synthesizer: Uses procedural Web Audio oscillators to play gentle acoustic cues (Bell for Medication, Saxophone for Meals, Marimba for Physical Therapy) without external asset streaming.

3. Offline Reliability (Service Worker)

Saksham uses a Service Worker to cache its core application shell. This allows full operation even in airplane mode or during network disconnections, ensuring routine cues and cognitive games remain uninterrupted.

4. User-Initiated Communication (WhatsApp & Email)

When the patient or caregiver chooses to dispatch an Emergency SOS or send clinical observation notes to Dr. Rajesh Verma, MD, the action is initiated through explicit user clicks via standard system URL schemes (tel:, wa.me, and mailto:). No background automated messaging occurs without user intent.
